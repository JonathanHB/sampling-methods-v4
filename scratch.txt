the fundamental issue with the WE+MTD approach is that WE wants 
a resampling time shorter than the correlation time in the starting well 
so that the walkers don't just slide back down within a single WE round, 
whereas MTD wants a resampling time at least as long as the correlation time
so that the walker distribution has time to adjust to the gaussian deposition
